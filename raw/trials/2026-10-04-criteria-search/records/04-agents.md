Rule used to choose what to read in this group: (1) every starting point the brief names, because the
brief asks for them; (2) sources that carry numbers about under-specified issues or over-specific tests
(benchmark annotation), because the brief asks for percentages; (3) official documentation or a widely
cited paper before a blog; (4) for any claim of the form "this improves what an agent ships", only a
source with a comparison counts as evidence, everything else is labelled argued or documentation.
