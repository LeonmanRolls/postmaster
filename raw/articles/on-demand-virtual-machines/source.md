Read for issue #341, to price machines that a ticket's run could use and delete afterwards, as an alternative to Cloudflare Containers or a monthly virtual private server. The pages are the providers' own pricing, billing and limits pages, their public price data (Vultr's and Linode's plan APIs, Azure's retail price API, Oracle's and AWS's price files), and their command-line tools' manuals. Google's prices come from a third party's mirror because its pricing pages could not be read. Only the passages the wiki relies on are kept, in `passages.md`.
