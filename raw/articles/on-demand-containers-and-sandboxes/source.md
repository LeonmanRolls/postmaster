Read for issue #341, to price the container a lane, a reviewer or a coachman would run in on the other platforms that sell one beside Cloudflare Containers, and to see which of them can hold a launch of the flow at all: the longest session, the largest size, the quota a new account starts with, and whether a program can start a process, stream its output and read its exit code. The pages are the providers' own pricing, billing, limits and lifecycle pages, their public price data (Azure's retail price API, AWS's price list, Fly's page source), and the repositories of the plugins that run a Herdr pane in a sandbox. Cloudflare's own rates were read again for comparison. Where a pricing page could not be read (Google's, Render's, Azure's), the entry says so and the rate is marked by how it was found. Only the passages the wiki relies on are kept, in `passages.md`. Prices are list prices for the region and currency each entry names, and they change.
